Classic (V1) dumps ship in the PC download:

  https://github.com/RetroCodeRamen/rebiko/releases/tag/v1.1026.4b

  roms/boot.bin    32 KB boot ROM (cyrom112.bin)
  roms/flash.bin   CyOS 1.2.46 (flash_v1246.bin)

Unzip and run. A git clone still does not contain the images — copy
them from the zip, or pass paths on the command line.

Xtreme (XT) dumps are not in the download. For T-Deck XT you still
need your own boot.bin (cyrom150.bin) and flash.bin (cyos_v1508.bin).

Dumps also show up on the Internet Archive:

  https://archive.org/details/cybiko
